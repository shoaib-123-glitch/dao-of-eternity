## Findings

- Daily goals add a steady but modest share of income, so skipping them is never punishing.
- **Sink needed:** Casual (15 min/day), Regular (45 min/day), Hardcore (3 h/day) end the month with over 1,000 unspent Spirit Stones. Once gear is finished, the only repeatable sink is elixirs. The planned **cave abode** upgrades should be the main long-term sink.
