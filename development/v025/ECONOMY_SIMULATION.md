## Findings

- Daily goals add a steady but modest share of income, so skipping them is never punishing.
- The cave abode (30 levels, 36,575 Spirit Stones in total) absorbs income for months; the abode rank column shows how far each player gets by day 30.
- No player type hoards more than 1,000 Spirit Stones by day 30.
